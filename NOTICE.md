# Notice and data availability

adapter, aggregate analyses, and experiment receipts designed to interoperate
with separately obtained JUDO, FREB-CAVER, and MMAD artifacts.

It does **not** redistribute:

- the JUDO base checkpoint;
- the FREB-CAVER native adapter;
- MMAD images, annotations, or row-level manifests;
- upstream evaluator source beyond the ECHO run entrypoint archived here;
- raw model generations containing dataset paths or question content.

No permission to use third-party artifacts is implied by access to this private
repository. Users must review the current terms of JUDO, Qwen2.5-VL, FREB-CAVER,
MMAD, GoodsAD, MVTec-AD, MVTec-LOCO, and VisA at their official sources.

The ECHO checkpoint is an experimental derivative artifact. It is not warranted
for industrial inspection, safety decisions, or production quality control.
